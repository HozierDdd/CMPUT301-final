## Vocabulary we use everywhere

Same word on every page, every issue and every diagram:

- **Lottery**: one listing by an owner with a registration period, a booking period and N winners.
- **Waiting list**: the pool of entrants who joined a lottery. Winners are drawn from it. It is not a stand-by queue.
- **Reservation**: what a winner gets after confirming; it can be cancelled by the entrant or rejected by the owner.
- **Availability**: a date range the owner has opened for booking.
